# Newspaper headlines (not included)

The Baden-Württemberg figure in the paper shows screenshots of three newspaper headlines. They are
not distributed here because the copyright lies with the respective publishers. The figure script
draws the figure without them; if you save your own screenshots under the file names below, they are
inserted at the same positions.

| file name | article |
|---|---|
| `bw_oettinger_headline.png` | „Oettingers Pizza-Connection", *DER SPIEGEL* 45/1993, 7 November 1993 |
| `bw_mappus_headline.png` | "Mappus overpaid for EnBW shares", ZEIT ONLINE (dpa), 21 November 2013 |
| `bw_afd_headline.png` | "Anger over flood of inquiries from the AfD", Stuttgart/Region (ST+), 30 May 2017 |
